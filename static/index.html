How it works

Three steps, zero accounts

You need "Manage Webhooks" permission in the channel. That's it.

1

Create a webhook

In Discord: Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook.

2

Copy & paste

Hit Copy Webhook URL and drop it in the box above. Pick everything or big moves only.

3

Get pinged

We check the tracker every 10 minutes and post to your channel the moment something changes.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no payment, no bot invite. It's a small fan project.

Where does the data come from?

From the public feed of PortWatch VI, a fan-made tracker. We just watch it and tell you when it changes. All credit for the tracking goes to them.

How do I stop the alerts?

Either click "Unsubscribe" above and paste the same URL, or simply delete the webhook in Discord. We detect deleted webhooks and drop them automatically.

What do you store?

Only the webhook URL, the mode you picked and when it last worked. No names, emails or IPs are kept.

Will it spam my channel?

No. Messages only go out when something actually changes, and pings are disabled. "Big moves only" keeps it to the headlines.

Does this link to game files?

Never. We don't host or link game files, keys or executables. We only relay progress from the tracker.

PortPing VI is an unofficial, fan-made alert relay. Not affiliated with PortWatch VI, Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Microsoft or Discord. No game files, keys or executables are hosted or linked. Progress data © its respective trackers via portwatch-vi.xyz.
